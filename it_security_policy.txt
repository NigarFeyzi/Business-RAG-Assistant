NovaTech IT Security Policy

Passwords:
Employees must use passwords with at least 12 characters.

Password Changes:
Passwords must be changed every 90 days.

Two-Factor Authentication:
Two-factor authentication is required for all company accounts.

Company Devices:
Employees must use company-approved devices for accessing confidential company information.

Lost Devices:
Lost or stolen company devices must be reported to the IT department immediately.

Software Installation:
Employees may not install unauthorized software on company devices.
