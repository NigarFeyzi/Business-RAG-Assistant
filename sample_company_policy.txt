NovaTech Employee Policy

Working Hours:
Employees work from 09:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday.

Annual Leave:
Employees are entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave per year.

Remote Work:
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.

Probation Period:
The standard probation period is 3 months.

Sick Leave:
Employees must notify their manager as soon as possible if they are unable to work due to illness.

Expense Reimbursement:
Approved business expenses are reimbursed within 10 business days after submission of valid receipts.
