NovaTech Business Travel Policy

Business Trips:
Employees may travel for approved business purposes with prior manager approval.

Accommodation:
The company reimburses hotel costs up to $150 per night.

Meals:
Employees receive a daily meal allowance of $45 during approved business travel.

Transportation:
Economy-class airfare and standard train tickets are reimbursed.

Receipts:
Receipts are required for expenses above $20.

Reimbursement Timeline:
Approved travel expenses are reimbursed within 10 business days.
